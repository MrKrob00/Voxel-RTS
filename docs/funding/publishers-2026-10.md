Nobody in this list states any sanctions or Ukraine restriction, but only Joygame's form (country dropdown) and Indie Fund's form were checked for country limits. ## Excluded and why

- **The Games Fund** - Russian capital (excluded per brief; not researched further).
- **HypeTrain Digital** - lists Pathologic 3 (Russian-founded Ice-Pick Lodge) and ownership/HQ not stated on its site; I could not clear the ownership question. Treat as unclear and avoid.
- **Brightika** - mobile publisher with USD 3M plan for PC/console indies (2025-05-14), but money covers marketing/UA/localisation not production; no submission channel found; ownership not stated (https://gamesbeat.com/brightika-charts-3m-expansion-into-pc-and-console-space/).
- **Digerati Distribution** - last listed releases in 2022 and a developer dispute reported; looks dormant.
- **Google Play Indie Games Fund 2026** - Sub-Saharan Africa only and closed 31 Jul 2026.
- **SCRYsoft Game Development Fund** - USD 100-300k grants for games over halfway, PC/console only (search result); wrong scale and stage.
- **Humble Games, Fellowship Entertainment** - a roundup says Fellowship is not accepting pitches; Humble not researched. Not listed.
- **PlayWay / Movie Games / Ultimate Games** - appear to work mainly with in-house incubated teams; no public pitch channel found.
- **Indie Fund's "acceptance"** is not guaranteed; it is a syndicate decision with no commitment to reply.
- **Mobile free-to-play publishers (Voodoo, Miniclip, Tilting Point etc.)** - not researched; a voxel strategy base-builder is not their usual intake and none was verified. Some mobile publishers have Russian-origin ownership, so do ownership checks before any contact.
