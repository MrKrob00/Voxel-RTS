# Castle Story reference images

Reference screenshots of the original Castle Story (Sauropod Studio, Unity) for comparing our map visuals. These are NOT game assets and must not be copied into the game. Files live in `images/reference/`.

Notes on reading these: block and height figures are estimates made by eye, using a Bricktron (the yellow worker, roughly 2 terrain voxels tall) as the yardstick. Images come from different builds. The Steam store shots and the `1.0` sandbox shots show the final look; "EarlyAccess 0.1.x" and "Prototype 0.0.x" shots (HUD text in the bottom-right corner) show older, flatter shading and denser, more saturated trees.

Sources: "Steam store" = store.steampowered.com/app/227860 (appdetails screenshot list). "Steam community" = steamcommunity.com/app/227860/screenshots (user screenshots, hosted on images.steamusercontent.com). "Fandom wiki" = castlestory.fandom.com (files hosted on static.wikia.nocookie.net).

## Synthesis: what the original maps look like

**Scale and shape.** Each map is one large irregular island (a ragged blob with bays and peninsulas, per the minimaps) plus a few tiny floating satellite islets, surrounded by open sea and cloud. The playable land is far larger than a unit: a Bricktron is only about 2 terrain voxels tall, and a single spawn clearing, a stockpile yard and a tower fit inside one meadow of roughly 20-40 voxels across, while the island holds dozens of such meadows. Island rims are sheer vertical drops straight into water with ragged underside voxels (no beaches or shore slope), and the visible island body is 10-25+ voxels deep.

**Relief.** The land is built from plateaus, mesas and terraces rather than continuous slopes. Meadow tops are very flat and smooth (the ground surface is shaded smoothly even though the terrain is voxel), and height changes happen at steep cliffs: typically 4-8 voxels for ledge steps, 8-12 for plateau sides, and 15-25+ for large rim and mountain walls. Cliff faces are dirt over stone strata with a checkerboard/diamond pattern of dark and light voxels; hills that are not cliffs are stair-stepped in diamonds and have grass caps. Chasms 10+ voxels deep with water or a stream at the bottom separate plateaus and are crossed by rope or wood bridges. Ramps are rare in the terrain (mounds and gentle slopes exist but most vertical movement is by cliff and built stairs).

**Cover and density.** Open ground is bright green lawn in 2-3 close shades laid out as organic blobs, with dirt borders where grass meets stone or cliffs and dirt pads around buildings and crystals. Ground cover is sparse in open meadow (tiny blades, small flowers) but very dense in fern thickets that edge the groves. Trees are pines in tight groups of roughly 8-40 trees, packed 1-3 voxels apart with hard edges, filling the tops of mesas, terrace ledges and the borders of clearings; the middle of each meadow stays empty. Snow appears only as white blob patches painted over the same green hills; no desert or full snow biome was seen in any of the 44 images.

**Resources.** Stone and ore are located in the cliffs, not on the meadows: grey stone strata inside the dirt cliff faces carry orange (copper-like) and pale blue/white ore voxels, either in thin horizontal veins or in clusters of 3-10 voxels, mostly visible on exposed cliff and pit walls. Loose boulder piles (white-grey) and small pebble scatters lie on grass near cliffs at forest edges, and orange or blue crystal clusters can sit on the lawn beside ore cliffs. Wood comes from the pine groves, which are placed near clearings. The minimaps show many small teardrop markers (resource deposits) spread over the whole island, not concentrated in one corner.

**Spawn and base.** The spawn crystal is a big blue crystal (tall shard cluster) on a flowery irregular dirt disc 4-5 voxels across at the middle of a flat open clearing, with the lawn level for 10-15 voxels around it. Pine groves and ferns ring the clearing a short walk away, ore cliffs are within view at one side, and the player starts with a handful of Bricktrons plus stockpile pads (wooden platforms about 3x2 voxels with dirt borders) placed beside the crystal; buildings sit on dirt skirts.
